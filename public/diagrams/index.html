1. User Payment & Escrow Collection Flow

End-to-end journey from checkout to Razorpay modal, cryptographic HMAC verification, 5% split, and escrow logging.

⬇ Download High-Res PNG (3200px) ⬇ Download Vector SVG
1

Checkout & Booking

Pet parent selects a service (Vet, Grooming, Training, Hostel) or cart products and clicks "Book & Pay Online".

POST /api/wallet-payouts/create-booking-order
2

Razorpay Order Creation

The backend calls Razorpay API to generate a registered order in paise (e.g. ₹1,000 = 100,000 paise) with a unique order ID.

orders.create({ amount, currency: 'INR' })
3

Customer Payment

The central Razorpay checkout modal opens, supporting UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), Cards, and NetBanking.

window.Razorpay(options).open()
4

Cryptographic Verification

The backend validates the cryptographic signature using HMAC-SHA256 with timing-safe buffer comparison.

HMAC-SHA256(order_id + '|' + payment_id, SECRET)
5

5% Split & Escrow

Total ₹1,000 is split: ₹50 (5% platform fee) and ₹950 (95% provider share held in temporary escrow).

Ledger: PAYMENT_COLLECTION (Status: SUCCESS)
6

Confirmation & Sync

Booking is confirmed, payment marked as Paid, and real-time notifications are dispatched to Customer, Provider, and Admin.

status: 'Confirmed', paymentStatus: 'Paid'

2. Service Provider Settlement & Payout Flow

How providers fulfill appointments, unlock escrow into cleared wallet balance, request withdrawal, and receive bank settlements with UTR.

⬇ Download High-Res PNG (3200px) ⬇ Download Vector SVG
1

Service Fulfillment

Provider conducts the appointment and updates the booking status to "Completed" on their dashboard.

PATCH /api/bookings/:id/status { status: 'Completed' }
2

Escrow Clearance

The net earnings (95% = ₹950) automatically unlock from Pending Escrow into the Provider's Available Payout Balance.

settlementStatus: 'Available'
3

Bank / UPI Verification

Provider registers bank account (Holder, A/C No, IFSC with auto-bank detection e.g. HDFC, SBI) or UPI ID.

POST /api/wallet-payouts/bank-account
4

Withdrawal Request

Provider requests withdrawal (min ₹500). System verifies balance and reserves funds to prevent double-spending.

POST /api/wallet-payouts/request-payout
5

Super Admin Audit

Super Admin and CRM Finance review KYC, booking fulfillment history, and bank account in the Admin Portal.

GET /api/wallet-payouts/admin/payouts
6

Disbursement with UTR

Admin transfers funds via banking network, enters the bank UTR, logs PAYOUT_DISBURSEMENT, and notifies provider.

POST /api/wallet-payouts/admin/process-payout (MARK_PAID)
